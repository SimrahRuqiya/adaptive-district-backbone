# adaptive-district-backbone

Capstone project: forecasting room occupancy 30 minutes ahead from environmental
and operational sensor data, to support energy-management decisions on an
Intel edge-to-cloud architecture.

## Dataset

[Room-level occupant counts and environmental quality from heterogeneous sensing
modalities in a smart building](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.9959417)

- 3 rooms: Room 1 (lecture, 84 seats), Rooms 2 and 3 (study zones, 32 seats)
- 44 separate days, all sensors present for every room
- Sensors: CO2, temperature, humidity, illuminance, VAV damper, camera occupancy
  and door entry/exit counts
- We use the `original` (non-gap-filled) version

## Getting the data

The `original` dataset files are included in `data/raw/`, and the cleaned table is
in `data/processed/model_table_5min.csv` (regenerated by `03_clean.ipynb`).
Source: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.9959417

## Key findings so far

- Sensors sample at different rates; temperature, humidity and VAV only log on
  change, so they are forward-filled within each day.
- Four room-days exceed capacity but are supported by CO2 rises, so they are kept
  and flagged as `event_day`. Other over-capacity readings are clipped.
- Occupancy is mostly zero, so models are compared against a persistence baseline
  and evaluated on occupied and changing periods, not just overall error.
- Final table: 36,432 rows x 58 columns, no missing values.

## Limitations

- 3 rooms in one building, 44 non-consecutive days.
- WiFi, lighting energy, setpoints, supply air and outdoor data are not in this
  dataset. Illuminance and VAV are used as substitutes.
- No energy data, so energy-management decisions are simulated.
